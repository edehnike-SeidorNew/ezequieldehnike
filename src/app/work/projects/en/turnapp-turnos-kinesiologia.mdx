## Overview

**Freelance project** built for a client. Currently **in development**. TurnApp lets patients of a physiotherapy clinic book appointments online: pick a day and time, reserve with a deposit and that is it, with no phone calls or waiting. The practitioner and the administrator see the full agenda.

## Features

- **"Book an appointment" calendar** that computes free slots from availability and blocked times.
- **Admin agenda** with day, week and month views.
- **Deposit booking**: the appointment starts as pending with an expiry, the payment is confirmed through a **Mercado Pago** webhook and only then becomes confirmed. A cron job releases expired pending appointments.
- **Login** with Google or email, automatic profile on sign-up and a flow to complete personal data.
- **Full demo mode** (patient and admin) to try it without configuring Supabase.
- *Glassmorphism* design over a blue gradient, in light and dark mode.

## Technologies

- **Next.js 16** (App Router), **TypeScript** and **Tailwind CSS 4**.
- **Supabase**: Auth, Postgres with RLS and versioned SQL migrations.
- **Mercado Pago**: payment preference and webhook.

## Technical decisions

- **Double booking prevented in the database**: an `EXCLUDE USING gist` constraint over the time range stops two active appointments of the same practitioner from overlapping, even if two people book at the same time.
- **RLS**: each patient only sees their own data, while the practitioner and the admin see the whole agenda.
- A trigger creates the profile on sign-up, taking first name, last name and phone from the registration.
