## Overview

**Freelance project** built for a client. Institutional website for a full-service law firm in Buenos Aires. The goal is to build trust and make contact easy: a visitor should understand which areas the firm covers and be able to write or message them on WhatsApp in a few clicks.

## Features

- **Sections**: areas of expertise, why choose us, track record with statistics, coverage map, team and an FAQ accordion.
- **Consultation form** with attachments. It first used FormSubmit and I later migrated it to a **Netlify Function** that sends the email through the **Resend** API, with branded design and without the limits of free plans.
- **Floating WhatsApp button** across all sections.
- **Responsive design** with a hamburger menu on mobile and a mix of serif and sans-serif typefaces for an institutional feel.

## Technologies

- **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**, no frameworks or build step.
- **Netlify Functions** (Node.js) and **Resend** for email delivery.
- Deployed on **Netlify**.

## Learnings

Moving email delivery to my own function meant handling environment variables, attachments and server-side validation. Choosing a static page resulted in a very light site that is easy to maintain.
